Tourist Highlights in 's-Hertogenbosch

Welcome tourists! Here are 3 must-see attractions when visiting our city:

1. St. John's Cathedral (Sint-Janskathedraal)

A beautiful Gothic cathedral built between 1370 and 1529. You can climb the tower for a great view over the city.

2. The Binnendieze Boat Tour

An underground canal network running beneath the historic city center. Guided boat tours are available daily.

3. Het Noordbrabants Museum

A museum showcasing art, culture, and history of North Brabant, including original paintings by Vincent van Gogh.
